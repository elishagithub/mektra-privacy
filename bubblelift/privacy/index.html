BubbleLift Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

Ark Code Solutions publishes BubbleLift. Contact us at sureshc5551@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Gameplay and local storage

The game stores scores, coins, fish discoveries, equipment, sound settings, and any chosen nickname on your device. You can remove local data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it.

Online leaderboard

BubbleLift uses Unity Authentication to sign players in anonymously for online rankings. Unity receives the anonymous player identifier, submitted best score, and optional nickname to maintain the leaderboard. Your score and public nickname can be visible to other players. Avoid using personal information in your nickname. Unity may also process IP addresses and technical information to operate and secure these services.

Advertising

BubbleLift for Android uses Unity LevelPlay to show interstitial ads between rounds. Unity and participating ad providers may process device or advertising identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location, app and device information, diagnostics, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. See Unity’s game player and app user privacy policy for more information and available privacy choices.

Score sharing

When you choose to share a score, BubbleLift passes your score text to the device’s share sheet. On Android this also includes the Google Play link. You choose the recipient and app. BubbleLift does not post your scores to social networks automatically.

Retention and deletion

Uninstalling the app does not remove online leaderboard records. To request deletion of your BubbleLift online profile and leaderboard data, email sureshc5551@gmail.com with the subject “BubbleLift data deletion.” Include your public nickname or player identifier if available. We may ask for additional information to verify and locate the record. Data may be retained where required by law.

Children and policy updates

BubbleLift is not directed to children. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us using the email above. We will update this page when our data practices change.
